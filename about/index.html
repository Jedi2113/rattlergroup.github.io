Our Start

Built from readiness, not retail.

Rattler Group started with a simple problem: most people who carry a radio have never been taught how to use one. Gear without skill is dead weight. So we built both — hardware that's ready out of the box, and training that turns a handset into a real tool.

We started small, outfitting radios for teams who needed reliable comms without a steep learning curve. As demand grew, so did the catalog — from single radios to fleet programming, from a single class to a full training track.

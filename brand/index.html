Brand Guide

A single reference for how Rattler Group looks, sounds, and behaves across every surface — grayscale, disciplined, built to work.

01 — Identity

Who We Are

Rattler Group believes knowledge is the key — that's what "Scientia aut mors" ("knowledge or death") means in Latin. We build and program two-way radio systems, provide custom programming services, and share the knowledge it takes to actually communicate over the air: prowords, phonetics, brevity, and net discipline. The brand reflects the product: no color, no clutter, no wasted motion. Every element earns its place.

02 — Logo

Marks & Usage

Do

Maintain clear space equal to the mark's height on all sides. Use on solid black or solid white only.

Don't

Don't recolor the mark, place it over busy photography without a scrim, or stretch its proportions.

05 — Voice

How We Sound

  • 01

    Direct

    Short sentences. No filler. Say the thing, then stop.

  • 02

    Precise

    Use the correct terms — prowords, phonetics, frequencies — and use them consistently.

  • 03

    Unadorned

    No exclamation points, no hype language. Confidence is quiet.
